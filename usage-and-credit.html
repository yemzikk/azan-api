Resources/Usage and credit

Usage and credit

The timetable data is in the public domain under CC0 1.0. Use it in your app, website, masjid display or bot. Credit is not required. A small credit with a link helps people check the times against the calendar they follow, and helps correct times reach more people.

Credit badge

HTML
<a href="https://azantimes.in/">
  <img src="https://azantimes.in/brand/azantimes-credit.svg"
       alt="Prayer times by AzanTimes" height="28">
</a>

On a dark background, use azantimes-credit-light.svg. Where a badge does not fit, a line of text works: Prayer times: AzanTimes (azantimes.in)

A few requests

  • Show the calendar’s name beside the times. It credits those who prepared it.
  • Please keep the logo’s letters and colours as they are, so people recognise them.
  • Keep your own app name, and present AzanTimes as the source of the times.
  • Tell us what you built at developers@azantimes.in. We can also let you know about changes to the API.
