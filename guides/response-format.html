Guides/Response format

Response format

Each timetable file starts with the location, the time zone and a suggested credit line, followed by days. Times mark the start of each prayer in 24-hour form, local to Kerala.

File fields

FieldTypeDescription
locationobjectid, name, type, district, school and source
timeZonestringAlways Asia/Kolkata
utcOffsetstringAlways +05:30
attributionstringA suggested credit line
yearintegerThe calendar year. Year files only.
linksobjectself, location, and the previous and next year
daysarrayOne entry per day, described below

Day fields

FieldTypeDescriptionExample
datestringISO 8601 date. Perpetual files use month and day instead.2026-11-23
fajrstringSubh (Fajr)05:05
sunrisestringSunrise, when Subh time ends06:28
dhuhrstringDhuhr, also called Zuhr or Luhr12:17
asrstringAsr. Hanafi timetables give a later time.15:34
maghribstringMaghrib18:03
ishastringIsha19:16

Examples are from the Kuttiady timetable for 23 November 2026.

Shafi’i and Hanafi timetables. Most timetables follow the Shafi’i school; 14 follow the Hanafi school and give a later Asr. On 23 November 2026, the Albusthan Thiruvananthapuram timetables give Asr at 15:34 (Shafi’i) and 16:26 (Hanafi). Read school on each location and show it, so people can pick the one their masjid follows.
